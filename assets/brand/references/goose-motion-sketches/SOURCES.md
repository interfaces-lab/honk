# Goose motion sketch references

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons on 2026-08-03. The local filenames are normalized for use as design references. Follow each source page for complete metadata and reuse terms.

## 01-step-by-step-goose.jpg

- What it shows: a 1921 construction lesson that builds a standing goose from a large triangle, then adds the neck, head, body contour, feet, and detail. The page also includes a swimming duck construction.
- Creator: Edwin George Lutz; scan supplied by Internet Archive Book Images.
- Date: 1921.
- License: no known copyright restrictions.
- [Source page](<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Drawing_made_easy_-_a_helpful_book_for_young_artists;_the_way_to_begin_and_finish_your_sketches,_clearly_shown_step_by_step_(1921)_(14583214499).jpg>)

## 02-winslow-homer-flight-study.jpg

- What it shows: a charcoal and white-chalk study of a wild goose in flight. The corrected head position remains visible, making this especially useful for studying neck alignment and forward motion.
- Creator: Winslow Homer.
- Date: 1896 to 1897.
- License: public domain.
- [Source page](<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Drawing,_Wild_Goose_in_Flight,_1896%E2%80%9397_(CH_18175163).jpg>)
- [Original file](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/da/Drawing%2C_Wild_Goose_in_Flight%2C_1896%E2%80%9397_%28CH_18175163%29.jpg)

## 03-werenskiold-flying-birds.jpg

- What it shows: a flock of geese drawn across several wing phases, useful as a compact study of upstroke, downstroke, overlap, and rhythm.
- Creator: Erik Werenskiold.
- Date: 1898.
- License: public domain.
- [Source page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Erik_Werenskiold_-_Flying_Birds_-_NG.K%26H.B.04848_-_National_Museum_of_Art,_Architecture_and_Design.jpg)

## 05-geese-ducks-motion-studies.jpg

- Date: between 1821 and 1891.
- License: CC0 1.0.
